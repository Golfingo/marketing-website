Powerful Features

Everything you need to get things done.

Persistent Golf Journey

Track achievements across every round and every course you play. Teed turns your golf history into an ongoing progression instead of a collection of isolated scorecards.

Achievement-Based Progress

Pars, birdies, and eagles all contribute toward completing unique achievement cards tied to every hole on the course. Every great shot moves your story forward.

Solo & Group Play

Build your own golf journey or connect rounds with friends through shared group cards. Celebrate milestones together while still tracking your personal progress.

Beautifully Simple Round Entry

Quick, intuitive score entry keeps the focus on golf, not statistics. Teed is designed to feel lightweight, rewarding, and easy to use after every round.
