Privacy Policy

Who We Are

This privacy policy covers Teed Golf, which is operated by Teed Golf LLC, together with the related website or services described here. It covers the app on iOS.

What Data We Collect

Information users provide directly may include:

  • Account and profile details such as Account identifier, Name, and Profile photo
  • Contact information people provide through the app or related services
  • Files, documents, or user-generated content handled through the app
  • Location data when people enable location-based features
  • Contact data used by contact-based features
  • Saved Golf Rounds. Comments on Rounds

Information collected automatically through the app or related services may include:

  • Crash logs, diagnostics, and reliability data
  • Usage and interaction data about how the app or related services are used
  • Identifiers needed to recognize an account, device, session, or subscription state

Additional data sent off the device may include:

  • Round Data
  • Teed Group Data
  • Round Comments

Account access may use sign-in methods such as Email and password, Sign in with Apple, and Google. Additional account details: we store a URL to the google profile photo only.

Some data leaves the device to support app functionality such as accounts, syncing, purchases, support, or other online features.

Some of this data may be linked to a person, account, or persistent identifier.

Information from Other Sources

We may also receive information from Social login providers when people choose to use those sources with the app or related services.

We receive profile information from Google Sign In.

How We Use Data

We use your data in a variety of ways, including:

  • To understand product usage and improve the app.
  • To find bugs and keep the app stable.
  • To personalize the app experience for the user.
  • To send Account or security alerts, Content updates, Marketing, Reminders, and Transactional messages.

The following providers are used:

  • Crash Reporting / Performance: Sentry. Used for crash Reporting and performance diagnostics.
  • Email / Messaging / Support: Amazon SES. Used for forgot Password Flow and marketing updates.
  • Website Hosting: Heroku. Used for hosting the Backend API some user facing web pages.

Additional feature details:

  • Location: Location used for Golf Course Search. User Location Lat/Long will be used to query nearby golf courses.

When Data is Shared

When an app feature needs online processing, syncing, account handling, or support, relevant data may be sent off the device to operate that feature.

Some data is shared with outside service providers that help operate the app or related services.

The following providers are used:

  • Crash Reporting / Performance: Sentry. Used for crash Reporting and performance diagnostics. Privacy policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/.
  • Email / Messaging / Support: Amazon SES. Used for forgot Password Flow and marketing updates. Optional for users. Privacy policy: https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/.
  • Website Hosting: Heroku. Used for hosting the Backend API some user facing web pages. Privacy policy: https://www.heroku.com/policy/security/.

Social Logins and Public Features

The app may let people register or sign in using Sign in with Apple and Google. When someone signs in this way, we may receive account or profile details such as Account identifier, Name, and Profile photo.

The app or related services include Public profiles, Public posts, and Comments. Information people post or share through these features may be visible to other users or to the public, depending on how the feature works.

User comments and profile information can be visible to everyone.

Data Retention

We keep data until the user deletes it, closes the account, or asks us to remove it, unless a longer retention period is legally required.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices and Rights

Some permissions are optional and are only used if the user chooses the feature that needs them.

Users can ask for deletion of their data using the contact or tools described below.

Where supported, users can ask for a copy or export of their data.

Where supported, users can request account closure or account-related deletion.

Privacy requests can be sent through support@teedapp.com.

Requests can be handled through Contact email, In-app deletion tools, and Manual support handling.

For users in certain US states, including California, applicable law may provide rights to know, access, correct, delete, or opt out of certain processing of personal information, subject to legal exceptions.

Children’s Privacy

This app is directed to children. Parents can contact us by email.

Third-Party Websites and Services

Comments, forums, and community areas on the related website may make submitted information visible to other users or to the public.

The following providers are used:
